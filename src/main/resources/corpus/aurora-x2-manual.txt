# Aurora X2 Espresso Machine User Manual
## 1. Overview
The Aurora X2 is a semi-automatic espresso machine for home kitchens. It has a 15 bar vibration pump, a
removable water tank that holds 1.8 litres and a thermoblock heater that is ready to brew about 40 seconds
after the machine is switched on. A steam wand on the right side froths milk. The machine draws 1450 watts
while heating.

Keep this manual. It describes daily use, cleaning, descaling and the meaning of the error codes shown on
the display.

## 2. First use
Wash the water tank with warm water and fill it with cold tap water up to the MAX mark. Insert the tank
until it clicks. Before making the first coffee, run two flushing cycles without coffee in the portafilter
to rinse the internal pipes.

Dip the supplied test strip into tap water for one second to measure water hardness. The number of red
squares gives the hardness level from 1 to 4. To store the level, hold the steam button for five seconds
and press the brew button until the display shows the matching number.
@page
## 3. Brewing espresso
Use 18 grams of finely ground coffee for a double shot and 9 grams for a single shot. Distribute the
grounds evenly and tamp them with firm pressure of about 15 kilograms. Lock the portafilter into the
group head and press the brew button.

A double shot should yield about 36 millilitres and the extraction should take between 25 and 30 seconds.
If the shot runs in less than 20 seconds the grind is too coarse and should be made finer. If it takes
longer than 35 seconds, grind coarser or tamp more lightly.

The default brewing temperature is 93 degrees Celsius. It can be adjusted between 88 and 96 degrees in
steps of one degree from the settings menu.

## 4. Steaming milk
Open the steam valve for two seconds to purge condensed water from the wand. Fill the jug to one third
with cold milk, place the tip just below the surface and open the valve fully. Stop when the milk reaches
60 to 65 degrees Celsius, which is when the jug becomes too hot to hold comfortably.

Wipe the wand with a damp cloth immediately after use and purge it again. Do not steam continuously for
more than 90 seconds, because the pump needs time to cool.
@page
## 5. Cleaning
Empty the drip tray when the red float rises above the grate. Rinse the portafilter and baskets under
running water after each session. Once a week, backflush the group head using the blind basket and one
cleaning tablet.

The drip tray and the water tank are not dishwasher safe and must be washed by hand. The heat of a
dishwasher warps the tank so that it no longer seals.

## 6. Descaling
Limescale builds up inside the thermoblock and must be removed regularly. The descale light blinks orange
when descaling is due, which at hardness level 3 is after roughly 200 shots.

Use Aurora descaler or dissolve 50 grams of citric acid in one litre of water. Never use vinegar: it
attacks the silicone seals and leaves a taste that is hard to rinse out. Pour the solution into the tank,
then hold both buttons for three seconds to start the programme. The programme takes about 25 minutes.

When it has finished, rinse the system by running two full tanks of fresh water through the machine.
@page
## 7. Error codes
The display shows a code when the machine detects a fault.

E1 means that the water tank is empty or not seated correctly. Refill the tank and push it in until it
clicks.

E3 means that the thermoblock has overheated. Switch the machine off and let it cool for 15 minutes.

E5 means that the pump is blocked, usually by limescale. Run the descaling programme.

E7 means that the temperature sensor has failed. The machine cannot be repaired at home in this case;
contact the service centre.

E9 means that the portafilter is not locked into the group head. Turn the handle firmly to the right.

## 8. Troubleshooting
If the coffee tastes sour, the water is too cool or the grind is too coarse: raise the brewing temperature
or grind finer. If it tastes bitter, lower the temperature or grind coarser. A shot without crema usually
means the beans are stale.

Water leaking around the portafilter during brewing indicates a worn group gasket. The gasket, part
number AX2-G58, should be replaced every 12 months.
@page
## 9. Energy saving and standby
The machine switches to standby 20 minutes after the last use. The delay can be changed to 60 or 120
minutes in the settings menu. In eco mode the thermoblock is kept at 60 degrees Celsius instead of being
switched off, which shortens the next heat-up to about 15 seconds.

## 10. Warranty and service
The Aurora X2 is covered by a warranty of two years for household use and six months for commercial use.
Damage caused by limescale is not covered, which is why regular descaling matters. Registering the machine
on the service portal within 30 days of purchase extends the warranty by one year.

Keep the original packaging. A machine sent for repair must be shipped in it, with the water tank emptied
and the drip tray removed.
