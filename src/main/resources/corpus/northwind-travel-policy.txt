# Northwind Travel and Expense Policy
## 1. Purpose and scope
This policy explains how Northwind Trading pays for business travel and what employees must do to be
reimbursed. It applies to all employees and to contractors whose agreement refers to it. The policy
takes effect on 1 March 2025 and replaces the 2022 edition. The finance team owns the document and
answers questions about how it should be read.

Travel should only be booked when a video call cannot achieve the same result. Travellers are expected
to spend company money as carefully as they would spend their own.

## 2. Booking travel
All flights, trains and hotels are booked through the Compass travel portal. Flights must be booked at
least 14 days before departure, because fares rise sharply in the last two weeks. Bookings made outside
the portal are reimbursed only if the portal was unavailable and a screenshot proves it.

Economy class is the standard for flights shorter than six hours. Premium economy may be booked for
flights longer than six hours. Business class requires the approval of a vice president and is normally
granted only for overnight flights followed by a working day.

For journeys of less than 400 km the train is preferred over flying, even if the ticket costs more,
because it reduces emissions and the time is usable for work.

## 3. Accommodation
Hotels are booked through the portal at the negotiated rate where one exists. The nightly hotel limit is
140 EUR in Tier 2 cities and 210 EUR in Tier 1 cities. The Tier 1 cities are London, Zurich, New York and
Singapore. The limit includes breakfast and local taxes.

A room above the limit requires written approval from the department head before the trip starts.
Approval given after the stay is not accepted. Employees who stay with friends or family instead of a
hotel may claim a gift allowance of 30 EUR per night without a receipt.

## 4. Meals and per diem
Meals are covered by a daily per diem instead of individual receipts. The per diem is 55 EUR for domestic
trips and 70 EUR for international trips. When a meal is provided by a host or included in a conference
fee, the per diem is reduced: by 20 percent for breakfast and by 30 percent each for lunch and dinner.

Alcohol is not reimbursed under any circumstances, including when it is part of a set menu. Client
entertainment is allowed up to 90 EUR per person, and the claim must list the names and companies of
everyone who attended.
@page
## 5. Ground transport
Public transport is the default way to move around a city. Taxis and ride-hailing services may be used
only when public transport is impractical, when carrying heavy equipment, or after 22:00.

Employees who use their own car are paid a mileage rate of 0.42 EUR per kilometre, up to 500 km per trip.
Longer distances must be covered by train or rental car. Rental cars are booked in the compact class, and
the tank must be refilled before the car is returned, since refuelling charges from rental companies are
not reimbursed. Parking fines and speeding tickets are never reimbursed.

## 6. Submitting expenses
Expense reports are submitted in the Ledgerly app and are due within 30 days of the return date. Reports
submitted later need the approval of the finance director. A receipt is required for every item above
25 EUR. A photograph of the receipt is sufficient as long as the date, the amount and the vendor are
legible.

If a receipt is lost, the traveller completes form TR-4, the lost receipt declaration. The form may be
used at most twice per calendar year. Approved amounts are paid with the next payroll run, which usually
means within 10 working days of approval.
@page
## 7. Approvals
Every trip must be approved before anything is booked. Trips with a total estimated cost below 1,500 EUR
are approved by the line manager. Trips above 1,500 EUR are approved by the department head. Trips that
cost more than 5,000 EUR, and all intercontinental trips regardless of cost, additionally need the
approval of the office of the chief financial officer.

The estimate includes transport, accommodation, per diem and any conference fees. Splitting one trip
into several requests to stay below a threshold is treated as a violation of this policy.

## 8. Corporate card
A corporate card is issued to employees who travel more than six times a year. The card has a monthly
limit of 3,000 EUR. It may not be used for personal purchases, even if the employee intends to repay
them. Cash advances taken with the card may not exceed 200 EUR per trip.

A lost or stolen card must be reported to the bank hotline within 24 hours and to the finance team on
the next working day. The employee is not liable for fraudulent transactions that are reported within
this period.
@page
## 9. Travel insurance and safety
Employees on approved trips are covered by the group travel insurance held with Helvia Assurance under
policy number NW-88417. The cover includes medical emergencies, baggage up to 2,000 EUR and the
cancellation of a trip for reasons outside the traveller's control. The emergency assistance line is
staffed 24 hours a day and its number is printed on the back of the corporate card.

Travel to destinations rated as high risk requires a security briefing at least five working days before
departure. The briefing is arranged by the facilities team.

## 10. Exceptions and violations
Exceptions to this policy are requested through the finance service desk and must include a business
justification. Repeated violations may lead to the withdrawal of the corporate card. Knowingly false
claims are a disciplinary matter. The policy is reviewed every two years.
